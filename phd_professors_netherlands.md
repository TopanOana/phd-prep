# PhD contacts in the Netherlands: security × sustainability

Prepared October 2026. Titles and affiliations were checked against university pages and recent vacancies where possible. Confirm on their staff pages before emailing.

**Finding anyone's papers (works for every entry below)**
- **dblp** (computer science bibliography; full chronological list with venues): https://dblp.org, then search the name
- **Google Scholar**: open their profile and sort by year; focus on the last 2–3 years
- **Semantic Scholar / arXiv**: often have free PDFs
- **University research portals** (often host open-access PDFs): research.ou.nl, research.vu.nl, research.tudelft.nl, research.utwente.nl

Tip: before emailing, read 2–3 of their papers from the last two years, and skim one recent PhD thesis from their group. It shows what a finished PhD there looks like.

## Professor 1: Prof. dr. ir. Harald Vranken

**Status:** Open position. SHARE PhD "Adaptive Privacy and Differential Privacy for Energy Data". He is co-supervisor and the contact person. Deadline: 1 November 2026.

**University:** Open Universiteit, Faculty of Science, Department of Computer Science. Also seconded part-time (since 2014) to the Digital Security group at Radboud University.

**Research group:** Security & Privacy research line, OU.

**Research overview:** Professor of Cyber Security since October 2023, focused on the technical side of cybersecurity. A prominent theme is the security and privacy of AI, as well as using AI to improve security: analysing internet traffic and detecting vulnerabilities in source code. Other topics include virtual security labs, (usable) security of online banking, and security of IoT communication protocols. Before moving to security, he worked on hardware testing (PhD at TU Eindhoven, then Philips Research). Co-applicant on the NWO-funded SHARE project.

# University of Twente: Sustainable Computing (SusCo) master's track leads

UT is launching a new Sustainable Computing specialisation in its Computer Science master's programme in the 2026–2027 academic year. It is driven by the three people below, from three research groups: Pervasive Systems (PS), Design and Analysis of Communication Systems (DACS) and Computer Architecture for Embedded Systems (CAES). It covers sustainability across the full computing stack: energy-efficient hardware and software, heterogeneous and distributed systems, sustainable networks, green AI, smart green cities and climate applications.

Why this matters for you: three groups launching a joint track signals where UT's computing research is heading. Future PhD positions on sustainable computing at Twente are likely to come from these groups.

- Programme page: https://www.utwente.nl/en/cs/programme/specialisations/sustainable-computing/
- Announcement: https://www.utwente.nl/en/eemcs/ps/news/2026/8/1125136/new-sustainable-computing-specialization-brings-together-expertise-from-ps-dacs-and-caes
