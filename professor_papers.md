# Papers by professor

Compiled October 2026 from dblp, university research portals and staff pages. Companion to `phd_professors_netherlands.md`; the numbering matches.

**How to read this list**
- ★ = read first. These are the papers most relevant to your profile or to what you'd pitch that person.
- **Free PDFs:** arXiv links and university repository links (Pure, DARE, staff pages) are free. IEEE Access is fully open access.
- **DOI links** go to the publisher. If a paper is paywalled, try "All versions" on Google Scholar, the author's university portal (Dutch universities make most papers open access), or simply email the author for a copy. That is normal and welcome.
- For papers without a link, paste the title into Google Scholar.
